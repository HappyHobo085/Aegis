! Title: Aegis abuse-TLD blocklist
! A small, curated list of throwaway top-level domains that are used almost
! exclusively for malvertising / rotating ad + scam networks and have negligible
! legitimate use. Piracy/streaming sites (the ad-block's primary target) serve
! their pop-under and banner ads from rotating, randomly-named domains on these
! TLDs (e.g. limbycocking.cfd, newsboydurance.cfd) — no static domain list can
! keep up with the rotation, but blocking the TLD itself does.
!
! `||tld^` is a host-anchor that matches any host ending in `.tld` (and NOT a host
! that merely contains the label, e.g. `cfd.com` is unaffected). It blocks across
! every Aegis tier: the matching engine, the WebKit content-filter converter, and
! the injected-JS domain set (suffix match) — see adblock_lists.rs.
!
! Intentionally conservative — these are the ShortDot/low-reputation gTLDs most
! associated with ad/malware abuse in 2024-2025 reports. TLDs with real mainstream
! use (.xyz, .top, .online, .site, .live, .club, .icu, .shop) are deliberately
! EXCLUDED to avoid breaking legitimate sites. The whole list is governed by the
! ad-block on/off toggle + per-site allowlist; extend via custom filters.
!
! This network rotates across throwaway TLDs — confirmed serving ads on streamex.sh

! new rotations are observed.
